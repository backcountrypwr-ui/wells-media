# Weekly strategy, week of Oct 5, 2026

Written Oct 5 from the Oct 4 snapshot (29 reels, last 90 days), the newest Instagram numbers, and a scan of top-performing powersports and mechanic reels from the last 30 days. YouTube has no videos yet, so everything below is Instagram data.

## Editing rules this week
1. **The first second is the whole game.** Reels where under 22% of viewers swiped away at the start got a median of 3,082 plays. Reels where over 40% swiped away got 482. Open on the problem or the result (the broken part, the nest, the engine firing), never on setup, a logo or a walk-up.
2. **Text on screen in the first frame.** Every outlier in the niche scan had a short line of text from frame one ("I can't sell this quad", "Hope this holds...", "Easy fix"). Keep it under 7 words, big, top third of the frame.
3. **Length 10–40 seconds.** Reels 15 seconds or shorter: median 2,825 plays. Reels over 60 seconds: median 1,037. Anything longer goes to YouTube.
4. **Cut on the action, no dead air.** Trim pauses and filler words; the best niche examples cut every 1–3 seconds during the repair steps.
5. **End with a reason to comment or save.** Comments are our weakest number (median 1 per reel). Close on a question ("What would you have checked first?") or the fix result.
