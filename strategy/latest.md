# Weekly strategy, week of Oct 12, 2026

Written Oct 11 from the Oct 11 snapshot (33 reels, last 90 days) and a scan of top-performing reels posted Oct 4–11. YouTube has no videos yet, so everything below is Instagram data.

## Editing rules this week
1. **Text on screen from the first frame, and open on the problem.** Reels where under 22% swiped away at the start: median 3,322 plays. Over 40%: 505. All three Oct 6 reels lost 55–61% at the start.
2. **Short only works with a hook.** The Oct 6 reels were 8–11 sec and still flopped. Aim for 15–40 sec with a story: problem, what was found, the fix. Never under 10 sec without on-screen text.
3. **Cap at 60 seconds.** Over 60 sec: median 1,043 plays. 41–60 sec: 2,711 (includes the clutch reel). Longer cuts go to YouTube.
4. **Cut on the action.** Trim pauses and filler; cut every 1–3 seconds during repair steps.
5. **End on a question or the result.** Median 1 comment per reel; 13 of 33 have none.
