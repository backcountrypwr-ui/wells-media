## Editing rules this week
1. **Put a question on screen.** "How would you remove this??" (52 sec) got 136,705 plays and 153 comments in about two days. The 8 new reels got 161 comments, after 2 the week before.
2. **Open on the stuck part or the problem itself.** Reels where under 22% swiped away at the start: median 4,373 plays (9 reels). Over 40%: 621 (19). The axle reel lost 19.0%.
3. **Cap at 60 seconds.** 41–60 sec: median 2,261 (10 reels). Over 60: 699 (5). The 90-sec transfer case removal lost 79.0% at the start.
4. **One version of each clip.** 5 trial copies this week got 147–368. "What do you think is going on?" went up 3 times in 4 minutes; the copies got 210 and 239.
5. **Raw sound when something gives.** The moment a stuck part lets go is the payoff; don't put music over it.
